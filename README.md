# Super Carry Bros.

Mario, but you are a VC. Three portfolio companies are your lives, term sheets are
your coins, and **TVPI is your score and your health at the same time**. Collect to
raise it. A Down Round Goomba marks you down. At zero, the company is gone.

**[Play it →](https://tmubashir.github.io/super-carry-bros/)**

## Controls

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| `←` `→` or `A` `D` | Move |
| `Space` (hold it) | Conviction jump — bigger check, further to fall |
| `Enter` | **The Daily Fund** — 4 companies, ~2 minutes, same seed for everyone today |
| `R` | Raise a round. Costs reserves, marks you up 2.5x, and every cent of it is air. |
| `X` | Secondary sale — sells the *mark* at 50%. Sometimes that beats holding. |
| `B` | Skip straight to the hot deal |
| `C` (end screen) | Copy your fund card — emoji portfolio, DPI, quartile — and go paste it somewhere loud |

## The one rule

**TVPI does both jobs.**

| | |
|---|---|
| term sheet | TVPI up |
| stomp an NPC | TVPI up |
| **Down Round Goomba** | **TVPI down** — it marks you down, that's what a down round does |
| TVPI hits zero | company gone, next life |
| Serial Pivot / falling anchor | kills you outright |

Every term sheet is simultaneously points and armour, so greed and safety are
priced in the same number. That's the whole economy — no reserves, no rounds, no
dilution, no DPI.

## The levels

**1-1 Demo Day** and **2-1 Sand Hill Road**. Each ends at a boss standing in front
of the signature: the **Tier 1 VC**, then the **debt holders**. Three stomps and
he's out of the deal; the flag is behind him. Beat both and you have returned the
fund.

The safe ground lane pays 1.4x. The sky lane — where both uncapped SAFEs, the
board seat, pro rata and 2&20 live — pays 2.6x. How high you climb is the game.

Power-ups keep their Mario jobs: board seat is a 1-UP, 2&20 is the star, **AI
ACCELERATION** makes you fast and lets you run straight through founders'
problems — and does nothing whatsoever about a falling anchor, because that is
the capital structure, not competition.

## Running it locally

One file. No build step, no dependencies, no package manager.

```
open index.html
```

Everything is drawn on canvas and the audio is synthesized at runtime, so
nothing is fetched from anywhere. Works offline, works from a `file://` URL,
works if you email it to someone.
